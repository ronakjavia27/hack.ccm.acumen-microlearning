
# What are the changes in recent ACLS update as compared to previous guidelines in all domains



The 2025 ACLS guidelines (effective October 2025, valid through 2030) represent refinements rather than a complete overhaul of the 2020 guidelines, with meaningful updates in defibrillation strategy, medication dosing, post-arrest care, and systems-level organization. [^1_1][^1_2][^1_3] Below is a comprehensive, domain-wise breakdown designed for rapid clinical recall and examination preparation.

## Unified Chain of Survival

| Domain | 2020 Guidelines | 2025 Guidelines | Clinical Rationale |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Chain structure** | Separate chains for adult/pediatric, in-hospital/out-of-hospital (4-6 links each) | Single unified 6-link Chain of Survival for all ages and settings | Simplifies teaching, reduces cognitive load, emphasizes universal principles [^1_3][^1_4] |
| **Links** | Varies by population | 1. Recognition/Activation 2. High-quality CPR 3. Defibrillation 4. Advanced resuscitation 5. Post-arrest care 6. Recovery | Adds explicit "Recovery" link addressing rehabilitation, survivor support, and rescuer mental health [^1_5] |

## High-Quality CPR

| Parameter | 2020 | 2025 | Evidence/Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Compression rate** | 100–120/min (adults) | 100–120/min (all ages, standardized) | Pediatric and adult rates now unified for simplicity [^1_3] |
| **Compression depth** | 2–2.4 inches (5–6 cm) adults; ~1/3 AP diameter children | Same (5–6 cm adults) | No change |
| **Chest recoil** | Complete recoil emphasized | Complete recoil emphasized | No change |
| **Interruption limit** | <10 seconds | <10 seconds (reinforced) | Stronger emphasis on minimizing pauses [^1_6] |
| **Rescue breaths** | Compression-only for lay rescuers in adult OHCA; 30:2 for trained providers | **Reversal of pandemic guidance**: Rescue breaths + compressions now recommended over compression-only for adult resuscitation (when trained providers available) | Pandemic-era PPE concerns no longer apply; ventilation matters especially in non-cardiac causes [^1_1] |
| **Dispatcher instructions** | Compression-only instructions for adult OHCA | Compression-only for adult OHCA; dispatcher-assisted CPR for pediatric arrests emphasized | No major change but pediatric emphasis strengthened [^1_7][^1_4] |
| **Newborn technique** | Two-finger or two-thumb both acceptable | **Two-thumb encircling hands technique now standard**; two-finger removed | Two-finger technique inadequate for depth [^1_1] |

## Vascular Access \& Medication Administration

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **IV vs IO** | IV preferred; IO acceptable if IV delayed | IV remains first-line; IO reasonable alternative if IV not feasible | No change in hierarchy, but IV preference reinforced [^1_7][^1_8] |
| **Endotracheal route** | Not recommended but mentioned | **Removed entirely from guidelines** | No evidence for efficacy; distracts from IV/IO [^1_8] |
| **Epinephrine timing** | 1 mg every 3–5 min | 1 mg every 3–5 min (no dosing change) | **Non-shockable rhythms**: Administer as early as feasible; **Shockable rhythms**: After initial defibrillation attempts fail [^1_7][^1_8] |
| **Atropine dose (bradycardia)** | 0.5 mg IV every 3–5 min (max 3 mg) | **1.0 mg IV every 3–5 min (max 3 mg)** | Dose doubled based on efficacy data [^1_7][^1_9] |
| **Amiodarone/Lidocaine** | Both acceptable for refractory VF/pVT | Both remain acceptable; timing refined | May be particularly useful in witnessed arrests with short drug administration time [^1_7] |
| **Magnesium** | Routine use not recommended | Routine use not recommended | No change |
| **Vasopressin** | May be considered; no advantage over epinephrine | No change | No change |
| **Sotalol** | Listed as antiarrhythmic option | **Removed from recommended antiarrhythmic infusions** | Limited evidence; potential pro-arrhythmic risk [^1_1] |

## Defibrillation \& Cardioversion

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Rationale |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Initial cardioversion energy (AF/AFL)** | Manufacturer-recommended (often 120–150 J biphasic) | **≥200 J for first shock** in atrial fibrillation/flutter | Lower energies associated with lower success and higher risk of degeneration to VF [^1_1][^1_8] |
| **Double sequential defibrillation (DSD)** | Not routinely recommended | **Should not be routinely performed**; remains rescue strategy only | DOSE-VF trial showed insufficient evidence for routine use [^1_7][^1_8][^1_5] |
| **Vector change defibrillation** | Not established | **Not established usefulness** | No change [^1_8] |
| **Polymorphic VT** | Treated as unstable | **ALWAYS unstable; immediate defibrillation without delay** | Clarified language to eliminate hesitation [^1_8] |
| **Refractory VF/pVT** | Standard algorithm | Minor refinements in escalation timing; DSD only as rescue | No major algorithm change [^1_2] |

## Airway Management

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Advanced airway choice** | Supraglottic or endotracheal based on provider skill and setting | No major change; either device acceptable if high likelihood of successful intubation | No change [^1_7] |
| **Ventilation rate with advanced airway** | 1 breath every 6 seconds (10/min) | No change | No change |
| **Ventilation before advanced airway** | 30:2 cycles | No change | No change |
| **Continuous compressions with PPV** | Reasonable when advanced airway in place | No change | No change [^1_7] |

## Bradycardia Algorithm

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Clinical Impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Atropine dose** | 0.5 mg IV | **1.0 mg IV** | Faster, more effective vagolytic effect [^1_7][^1_9] |
| **Escalation** | Atropine → pacing/infusions | **Reinforced prompt escalation** when bolus medications fail; algorithm diagram revised | Reduces delay in definitive therapy [^1_2] |
| **Stable vs unstable thresholds** | Clinical judgment | **Tightened thresholds** for distinguishing stable vs unstable | Reduces hesitation on when to escalate [^1_4] |

## Tachycardia Algorithm

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Stable vs unstable** | Clinical judgment | **Clearer criteria** for stable vs unstable tachycardia | Reduces decision paralysis [^1_6][^1_4] |
| **Synchronized cardioversion energy** | Manufacturer-recommended | **Refined dose recommendations**; ≥200 J for AF/AFL | Aligns with defibrillation update [^1_2][^1_8] |
| **Medications** | Adenosine, beta-blockers, calcium channel blockers, amiodarone | Minor adjustments; **sotalol removed** | No major change otherwise [^1_1] |

## Post-Cardiac Arrest Care

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Evidence/Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Temperature management** | Targeted temperature management (TTM) 32–36°C for 24 hours | **Active fever prevention and controlled normothermia**; temperature control 32–37.5°C for **at least 36 hours**; immediate treatment of fever >37.5°C | Shift from routine hypothermia to fever prevention; continuous temperature monitoring mandatory [^1_3][^1_10] |
| **Oxygenation (post-ROSC)** | SpO₂ 92–98% (or 94–98% in some guidance) | **SpO₂ 92–98%** (inclusive) | No change; avoid both hypoxia and hyperoxia [^1_7][^1_5] |
| **Blood pressure targets** | MAP ≥65 mmHg or SBP ≥90 mmHg | Refined hemodynamic goals; avoid hypotension | No major change but emphasis on individualized goals [^1_2] |
| **Neuroprognostication** | Multimodal assessment recommended; avoid early prognostication | **Multimodal neuroprognostication** emphasized; timing guidance refined | No change in principle; stronger evidence against premature prognostication [^1_7][^1_5] |
| **Recovery** | Post-arrest care as distinct phase | **Recovery integrated into Chain of Survival**; includes physical, mental, social support; survivor rehabilitation before discharge | Explicit recognition of long-term recovery needs [^1_4][^1_5] |

## Termination of Resuscitation (TOR)

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **TOR criteria** | BLS and ALS TOR rules | **Stratified by EMS provider level**: BLS TOR, ALS TOR, Universal TOR (UTOR) | Better alignment with scope of practice [^1_8] |
| **ETCO₂ use** | ETCO₂ <10 mmHg after 20 min may support futility | **Do NOT use ETCO₂ in isolation** to terminate resuscitation | ETCO₂ is one data point, not sole criterion [^1_8] |
| **Head-up CPR** | Experimental | **Should NOT be used outside rigorous clinical trials** | Insufficient evidence; potential harm [^1_8] |

## Special Populations

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Pregnancy** | Maternal resuscitation prioritized; perimortem C-section considered | No major algorithm change; **fetal monitoring during maternal arrest not recommended**; TTM and fetal monitoring post-ROSC with OB support | No change in core principles [^1_7] |
| **Opioid overdose** | Naloxone in BLS | **Naloxone explicitly in BLS algorithm** for suspected opioid-associated emergencies | Clarified placement [^1_4] |
| **Pediatric IHCA** | E-CPR not routinely recommended | **E-CPR may be considered for select pediatric IHCA** as rescue therapy when conventional CPR failing | Pediatric-specific refinement [^1_7] |

## Stroke Algorithm

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Thrombolytics** | tPA within 3–4.5 hours | **Addition of thrombolytic agent in stroke algorithm** (tenecteplase recognized as alternative) | Expands thrombolytic options [^1_2] |
| **Imaging** | CT/MRI within 20 minutes | No change | No change [^1_7] |
| **Endovascular thrombectomy** | Within 6 hours (select cases up to 24 hours) | No change; transfer within 3 hours if EVT not on-site | No change [^1_7] |

## Team Performance \& Systems

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Closed-loop communication** | Recommended | **Stronger emphasis** woven through all algorithms | No change in principle; reinforced [^1_6][^1_4] |
| **Real-time CPR feedback** | Recommended | **Enhanced guidance** | Technology integration emphasized [^1_4] |
| **Debriefing** | May benefit rescuer mental health | **Explicit recommendation** for post-resuscitation debriefing for lay rescuers, EMS, and hospital workers | Mental health support formalized [^1_7][^1_5] |

## Diagnostic Adjuncts

| Domain | 2020 | 2025 | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **ETCO₂ monitoring** | Recommended for CPR quality and ROSC detection | No change; **not for isolated TOR decisions** | Continues as quality metric [^1_7][^1_8] |
| **POCUS** | May be considered by experienced providers | **Only if performed without interrupting CPR** | Reinforces no-interruption principle [^1_8] |
| **Continuous arterial BP monitoring** | Can improve CPR quality | No change | No change [^1_7] |

## Summary of High-Yield Changes for Examinations

| Category | Must-Know Change |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Atropine dose** | 1 mg IV (was 0.5 mg) [^1_7][^1_9] |
| **Cardioversion energy** | ≥200 J initial for AF/AFL (was 120 J) [^1_1][^1_8] |
| **Temperature management** | Fever prevention 32–37.5°C for ≥36 hours (shift from TTM 32–36°C) [^1_3] |
| **Rescue breaths** | Compression + ventilation now recommended over compression-only (reversal of pandemic guidance) [^1_1] |
| **Endotracheal drugs** | Removed from guidelines entirely [^1_8] |
| **Sotalol** | Removed from antiarrhythmic infusions [^1_1] |
| **DSD** | Not routinely recommended; rescue only [^1_7][^1_8] |
| **Chain of Survival** | Unified 6-link chain (added Recovery link) [^1_3][^1_5] |
| **TOR rules** | Stratified by provider level; ETCO₂ not for isolated use [^1_8] |
| **Newborn compressions** | Two-thumb technique only (two-finger removed) [^1_1] |

These updates reflect incremental evidence-based refinements rather than paradigm shifts, with the strongest clinical implications in defibrillation energy selection, atropine dosing, post-arrest temperature strategy, and the explicit integration of recovery into resuscitation systems. [^1_2][^1_10][^1_5]
